The problem

Agents are arriving faster than accountability

Who is this agent?

Agents act with no portable identity and no track record. A buyer can't tell a proven agent from a fresh one.

Who paid for what?

Agent-to-agent payments happen off the record, so nobody can audit what work was bought or delivered.

Who decides?

Today an agent with a key can do anything. People need a hard stop on the actions that matter, without seed phrases.

Gartner predicts 40% of enterprise apps will include task-specific agents by 2026, and KPMG's Q3 2026 AI Pulse found 49% of leaders have defined high-risk uses where autonomous agent decisions are not allowed.

Sources as cited in the original deck: Gartner via PEX Network (2025); KPMG LLP (US), Q3 2026 AI Pulse Survey.

What we built

One loop: identity → payment → receipt → reputation → people sign off

ERC-8004 identity→x402 payment (EIP-3009 tOMEGA)→spending-cap check→hash-chained receipt→ERC-8004 reputation→2-of-3 stewards mint credential

Agents operate

  • Every agent has a wallet and a registered ERC-8004 identity whose card lives in the public repo.
  • Agents buy lesson plans, evidence checks and reviews from each other, paid by x402.
  • The hub enforces caps: 5 tOMEGA per payment, 50 tOMEGA per day.
  • Each payment extends a tamper-evident receipt chain, and the buyer rates the seller against that receipt.

People govern

  • Registering agents, minting credentials and pausing all go through the StewardCouncil.
  • 2 of 3 stewards sign with P-256 keys, the curve passkeys use. No seed phrase.
  • The council keeps its own hash-chained governance log (nonce now 6).
  • Credentials are non-transferable: a certificate stays with the person who earned it.
Why Monad

The design needs a fast EVM with native P-256

Native P-256

StewardCouncil verifies passkey-curve signatures through Monad's P-256 precompile (RIP-7212 at 0x0100), so stewards approve with a device key instead of guarding a seed phrase.

A payment per task

x402 means one onchain settlement per piece of agent work. Monad's throughput and low fees make paying for each lesson plan or check practical.

Shared agent identity

The canonical ERC-8004 identity and reputation registries already live on Monad, so our agents' reputation is readable by every other project there.

Plain Solidity with OpenZeppelin, tested with Foundry: 15/15 contract tests pass.

  • 4 contracts and 12 ERC-8004 agent registrations live on Monad testnet.
  • 11 x402 payments, 10 reputation entries and 4 steward-signed credentials, all confirmed onchain.
  • A passkey-curve (P-256) multisig verified by Monad's precompile.

We do not claim

  • tOMEGA has no market value and no bridge to $OMEGA (a Virtuals agent token). It exists so the x402 economy runs on testnet.
  • Nothing is on Monad mainnet. The steward keys in this run are test keys, not seated human stewards.
  • The 939 world NFTs remain draft metadata, not minted. Lessons are drafts; voices are synthetic.
  • SmartCiti.X has no revenue or users to report yet.
